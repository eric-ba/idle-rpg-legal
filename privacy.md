# Idle Ladder — Privacy Policy

_Effective {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}_

Idle Ladder ("the game", "we") is a mobile game for iOS, offered in the United States and run by
an independent developer. This policy says
what the game collects, why, and what you can do about it. Questions: eric@broadarrow.tech.

## What we collect

| Data | Where it comes from | Why |
|---|---|---|
| A sign-in identifier from Apple or Google | Sign in with Apple / Google | To know which account is yours. We receive an opaque ID only — not your email address, name or contacts. |
| Your character name | You choose it | Shown to other players on leaderboards, profiles and the marketplace. |
| Game data: progress, items, gold, fights, marketplace listings and sales | Playing the game | To run the game. |
| Reports and blocks you make, with any note you write | You submit them | To moderate the game. |
| IP address and request details in server logs | Your device's connection | Security, abuse prevention and fixing errors. |

We don't collect your location, contacts, photos or advertising ID. The game has no ads, no
third-party analytics and no tracking across other apps or websites. Reminders are local
notifications scheduled on your device; we don't receive a push token.

## Who can see it

- **Other players** see your character name, class, level, progress, trophies and marketplace
  listings. Nothing else.
- **Service providers** that host the game for us (DigitalOcean, servers in the United States) store
  the data on our behalf. We don't sell or share your data with anyone else, except when required
  by law.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data while your account exists. Server logs are kept for up to 30 days. Backups are
kept for up to 30 days, so deleted data leaves the backups within 30 days of deletion.

## Deleting your account

You can delete your account in the game: **Settings → Delete account**. This removes your sign-in
identifier, replaces your character name, takes your items and gold out of the game and ends your
sessions. Some game records (for example, a record that an item was sold) stay for auditing but are
no longer linked to you. Signing in again afterwards starts a new account.

You can also ask us at eric@broadarrow.tech for a copy of your data or to correct it.

## Children

The game is for people aged 13 and over. We don't knowingly collect data from children under 13.
If you believe a child under 13 has an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

## Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, tell you in the
game.
